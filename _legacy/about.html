A quiet house by the kayal

Kayal Breeze is a homestay of rooms and flats, built and run by SK Group near Varapuzha Post Office, on the northern edge of Kochi.

The house

Three floors, white and grey, under deep eaves.

Kayal means backwater in Malayalam, and Varapuzha is where the Periyar opens into them: small islands, ferries and fishing nets a few minutes from the city's highways.

The house was built for stays of a night or of several months. There are suite rooms with their own sitting room, deluxe rooms in AC and non-AC, and 2BHK and 3BHK flats with a full kitchen and a dining table.

Every room is finished in the same materials: teak furniture, marble-look floors veined in gold, zebra blinds, white linen and a slatted wood ceiling framed in cove light.

At the side of the house a gravel garden runs to a water wall with a lit Buddha. It is the quietest place on the plot, and the best one after dark.
