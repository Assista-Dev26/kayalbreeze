Kayal Breeze

A homestay of rooms and flats beside the Varapuzha backwaters, Kochi.

Enquire on WhatsApp

Kayal is Malayalam for backwater. Ours is a three-storey house near Varapuzha, where the Periyar spreads around the Kadamakudy islands.

Inside: teak, marble floors veined in gold, white linen and cool rooms. Outside: a garden path that ends at a water wall and a lit Buddha.

Rooms by the night, flats by the month. Availability and rates are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

Teak and marble, lit softly.

Every room is finished the same way: teak furniture, a slatted wood ceiling framed in cove light, marble-look floors and zebra blinds.
